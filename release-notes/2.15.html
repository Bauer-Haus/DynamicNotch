A bigger open notch

  • The open notch now grows to twice the width of your Mac's notch, so opening it feels like a real expansion and long song titles have more room. To keep Apple's original size, choose Apple in Settings › Notch › Open width.
  • The waveform in the open notch is now the same size as in the closed notch, so it glides into place when the notch opens and closes.

Smoother opening and closing

  • The player comes into focus as the notch opens, just as it blurs away when it closes.
  • Opening the notch again right after closing it no longer leaves the album cover and waveform behind, then snapping into place.
  • The open notch keeps its size when you connect a display or wake your Mac.

Album covers

  • Songs in Apple Music playlists that came without a cover now show the right album art, instead of the previous song's cover or the Music icon.
