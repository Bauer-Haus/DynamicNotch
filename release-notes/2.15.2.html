Screen corners

  • The rounded bottom screen corners now match each MacBook's own screen. DynamicNotch reads the corner size macOS keeps for your model and screen size, so 13″, 14″, 15″ and 16″ MacBooks all get the right curve.

Settings

  • Music: "Closed notch" is now "Collapsed notch", and "Closed waveform" is now "Collapsed waveform".
  • The waveform always shows the album's colours, and the open notch always has its soft shadow, so those two switches are gone.
