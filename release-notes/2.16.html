Full-screen visualizer

  • A new full-screen visualizer: slow, flowing light in the colours of the album cover that moves with the music. It speeds up as songs get louder, flashes on the beat and sends ripples out from the cover. Each side of the screen follows its own side of the music.
  • Open it by clicking the waveform in the open notch, choosing Open Visualizer from the menu bar menu, or adding the Visualizer button to the notch's controls in Settings.
  • Move the mouse to show the song and playback controls. Esc closes it, Space plays and pauses, the arrow keys skip songs and A shows or hides the album cover.

Speaker pulses

  • Pulses of light show where the music would come from on a speaker system. Choose 2.0, 2.1 or 5.1 at the top of the visualizer (or press S), or turn them off.
  • 2.0 shows the left and right speakers, each following its own side of the music.
  • 2.1 adds a subwoofer pulse behind the album cover that plays the deep bass, up to 80 Hz like a home subwoofer.
  • 5.1 places the speakers like a home theatre: front left and right play the mids, the centre the highs, the rear speakers the room and reverb of the recording, and the subwoofer the bass.
  • The pulses kick on drum hits and other sharp sounds, and adapt to each song, so loud recordings still pulse rather than glowing steadily.
