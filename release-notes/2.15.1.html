Album covers

  • With Apple Music as the music source, songs now show their own album cover. Before, a song could keep the previous song's cover, or show the wrong one.

Notch

  • The wider open notch (Match notch) is now the same size on every Mac, just as Apple's island is the same size on the 14″ and 16″.
  • New in Settings › Music: Closed waveform. Choose Still until hovered to keep the waveform still until you point at the notch, like Apple's MacBook island. Always moving is still the default.
