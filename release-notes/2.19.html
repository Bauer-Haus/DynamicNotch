Album Flow

  • Album Flow now follows the music: when a song from another album in your library starts, it turns to that album, and a speaker icon by the album's name shows it's the one playing. If you've turned a cover over to read its songs, it waits rather than pulling you away.
  • Albums are found even when Now Playing names them differently from your library (without " - Single", with other accents, or with a featured artist).
  • Album Flow no longer closes by itself the first time it opens.
