Speaker pulses like a home theatre receiver

  • 5.1 now plays the music the way a receiver spreads stereo over five speakers: the centre lights for what's in the middle of the mix (vocals, kick, snare), the front left and right for what's only on their side, and the rear speakers for the room and reverb. A mono recording lights just the centre, as it would at home.
  • New crossovers: the fronts play from 40 Hz, the centre from 60 Hz, the rear speakers from 70 Hz, and the subwoofer below 50 Hz. 2.1 uses the same fronts and subwoofer.

AirPods delay

  • AirPods and other Bluetooth headphones play the sound a moment late, so the visualizer could run slightly ahead of what you hear. Click the AirPods button at the top of the visualizer (or press D) to hold it back, and pick 100 to 300 ms to match your headphones. It's off unless you turn it on.
