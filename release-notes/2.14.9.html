Software updates

  • DynamicNotch now updates itself. It checks once a day, and you can check any time with Check for Updates… in the menu bar menu or in Settings › About.
  • Updates are signed and notarized, and install with one click.

Waveform

  • The waveform keeps moving when your AirPods connect, move to your iPhone and back, or when a notification arrives.
  • Fixed a freeze that could stop the waveform until DynamicNotch was restarted.
