Album Flow

  • Browse the albums in your Music library full screen: the front cover faces you, the rest stand in two stacks turned towards it, with reflections on a black floor. It opens on the album that's playing.
  • Swipe or scroll, drag, click a cover, use the arrow keys, or type an artist's name to move through your albums. The scroll bar at the bottom takes you anywhere.
  • Click the front cover to turn it over and see its songs; click a song to play from it. Double-click the front cover, or press Return, to play the whole album in Music. Space plays and pauses, Esc closes.
  • Open it from the menu bar menu, or add the Album Flow button to the notch's controls in Settings. The first time, macOS asks to let DynamicNotch read your Music library.
  • Albums are played through a playlist named "Album Flow" in your library, since Music can't be told to play an album directly.
